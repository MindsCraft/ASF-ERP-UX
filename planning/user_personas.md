# User Personas & Roles

**Project:** ASF ERP System
**Source:** SRS Section 2.5

## 1. Super Administrator
*   **Count:** 1
*   **Role:** System Owner / Top-Level Admin
*   **Key Responsibilities:**
    *   **User Management:** Create, Edit, Delete ALL users. Manage Roles & Permissions (including Custom Roles).
    *   **Configuration:** Biometric settings, Salary templates, PF rules, Tax settings.
    *   **Global Access:** Full control over Employees, Payroll, Leave, Attendance, Expenses, Policy, Notices, and Reports.

## 2. HR Administrator
*   **Count:** 1
*   **Role:** Primary Operator of the HRM Module
*   **Key Responsibilities:**
    *   **Onboarding:** Add new employees, upload documents (CV, Image, NID, Bank Info).
    *   **Employee Mgmt:** Edit info, Update status, View complete profiles.
    *   **Operations:** Generate/Download Attendance reports, Manage Leave, Process Payroll.
    *   **Claims & Notices:** Verify expense claims, Manage Notice Board.

## 3. HR Manager / Line Manager
*   **Count:** Unknown
*   **Role:** Team Leader / Supervisor
*   **Key Responsibilities:**
    *   **Team Oversight:** View direct report lists and profiles.
    *   **Approvals:** Approve/Reject Leave applications and Expense claims for the team.
    *   **Monitoring:** View team attendance summaries and generate related reports.

## 4. HR Officer
*   **Count:** Unknown
*   **Role:** Support Staff / Junior HR
*   **Key Responsibilities:**
    *   **Team View:** View employee lists and basic profiles (Read-Only/Limited).
    *   **Attendance:** View detailed attendance logs.
    *   **Self-Service:** Submit own claims, view notices.
    *   **Reporting:** View reports based on specific role permissions.

## 5. Accounts / Finance Officer
*   **Count:** Unknown
*   **Role:** Financial Controller
*   **Key Responsibilities:**
    *   **Payroll Processing:** Process monthly salary, verify calculations, manage Arrears/Deductions.
    *   **Disbursement:** Execute payments (Bank, Cash, Cheque), Upload Bank Templates.
    *   **Documents:** Generate and Email Payslips, Tax Reports, PF Reports.
    *   **Claims:** Final Review and Approval of expense claims.
    *   **Funds:** Manage Employer & Employee PF contributions.

## 6. Employee (General User)
*   **Count:** Unknown
*   **Role:** Standard End User
*   **Key Responsibilities:**
    *   **Leave:** Submit applications, View Balance & History.
    *   **Attendance:** View own Attendance percentage and logs.
    *   **Expenses:** Submit claims with proof uploads.
    *   **Profile:** View own limited profile, Change Password.
    *   **Info:** Download Salary Slips, Read Admin Notices.
