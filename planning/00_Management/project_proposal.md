## 1. Executive Summary
The As-Sunnah Foundation (ASF) requires a robust, scalable, and user-centric Human Resource Management (HRM) module. To ensure the system meets the high standards of the foundation's workforce, a comprehensive UX design and strategic planning phase is critical before any development begins.

**Mindscraft.dev** proposes a design-first approach, focusing on creating intuitive interfaces, seamless user journeys, and a cohesive design system that ensures high adoption and efficiency across all institutions and branches.

## 3. Design Methodology
**Mindscraft.dev** utilizes a modern, user-centric design methodology to ensure the system is both functional and delightful to use.

*   **Discovery & Research:** In-depth analysis of user personas and organizational requirements.
*   **Information Architecture:** Comprehensive mapping of system structure and navigation flows.
*   **Wireframing:** Low-fidelity structural blueprints to validate logic and layout before high-fidelity design.
*   **Design System:** Creation of a scalable visual language (Typography, Colors, Components).
*   **Usability Alignment:** Continuous verification against functional requirements (SRS).

---

## 4. Design & Planning Milestones
The project emphasizes a rigorous discovery and design process:

*   **Requirements Mapping:** 100% alignment between functional needs and user experience.
*   **Information Architecture:** Complete sitemap and navigation structure definition.
*   **Wireframing (Completed):** Low-fidelity blueprints for 100% of core modules to ensure UX excellence.
*   **Market Analysis:** Comparative research against global HRM standards to ensure best-in-class UX.

## 6. Risk Mitigation & Quality Assurance

### 6.1 Potential Risks & Mitigation Strategies
| Risk | Impact | Probability | Mitigation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope Creep** | High | Medium | Clear change request process with additional cost estimates |
| **Client Feedback Delays** | Medium | Medium | Built-in buffer time and structured review cycles |
| **Technical Constraints** | Medium | Low | Early technical feasibility assessment with development team |
| **Resource Availability** | High | Low | Dedicated team allocation with backup resources identified |

### 6.2 Quality Assurance Process
- **Weekly Progress Reviews:** Regular client check-ins with deliverable previews
- **Iterative Design Process:** 2 revision rounds included per phase
- **Cross-functional Validation:** Technical feasibility review at each milestone
- **User-Centered Testing:** Prototype validation with target user groups

### 6.3 Client Collaboration Framework
- **Communication:** Weekly status calls + Slack/Email for daily updates
- **Review Process:** 48-hour turnaround for client feedback on deliverables
- **Approval Gates:** Formal sign-off required before proceeding to next phase
- **File Sharing:** Figma workspace access for real-time design collaboration
