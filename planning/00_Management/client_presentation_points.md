# Client Presentation Key Points

## Opening Hook (30 seconds)
"The difference between a successful HRM system and a failed one isn't the features—it's whether employees actually want to use it. That's where UX design becomes critical."

## Problem Statement (1 minute)
- **Current Challenge:** Complex HRM systems often have low adoption rates (typically 40-60%)
- **Business Impact:** Poor UX leads to training costs, resistance, and reduced productivity
- **ASF Specific:** Foundation needs intuitive system for diverse user skill levels across institutions

## Solution Overview (2 minutes)
- **Design-First Approach:** Plan the experience before building the system
- **Comprehensive Coverage:** All 11 modules designed with user-centric methodology
- **Proven Process:** Research → Wireframes → Visual Design → Prototype

## Key Differentiators (1 minute)
- **Foundation Sector Expertise:** Understanding of organizational structure and user needs
- **Scalable Design System:** Consistent experience across all modules and future features
- **Risk Mitigation:** Prevents costly redesigns during development phase

## Investment Justification (2 minutes)
- **73 Man-Days Investment:** Comprehensive coverage ensuring nothing is missed
- **13-Week Timeline:** Structured phases with clear milestones and deliverables
- **ROI Calculation:** UX investment saves 40-60% development time and costs

## Success Metrics (1 minute)
- **Measurable Outcomes:** 85% usability score, 90% client approval rate
- **Quality Assurance:** Built-in review cycles and iteration rounds
- **Timeline Commitment:** Weekly progress updates and milestone deliveries

## Closing & Next Steps (1 minute)
- **Immediate Value:** Start with requirements analysis and user research
- **Clear Process:** Structured phases with approval gates
- **Partnership Approach:** Collaborative design process with ASF team involvement

## Anticipated Questions & Responses

**Q: "Why not start development directly?"**
A: "UX design prevents expensive changes later. It's 10x more costly to fix UX issues after development than during planning."

**Q: "Can we reduce the timeline?"**
A: "We can discuss phase prioritization, but quality UX requires proper research and iteration time."

**Q: "What if requirements change?"**
A: "Our iterative process includes revision rounds, and we have a clear change request process for scope adjustments."

**Q: "How do we measure success?"**
A: "We'll conduct user testing on prototypes and measure against specific KPIs like task completion rates and user satisfaction scores."
